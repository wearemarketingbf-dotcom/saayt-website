You've opened a new branch. The sign is up and the team is ready, but people searching on Google Maps can't find it, or they find it under a different name from your other branches. Adding a branch on Google is an operational step, like putting up the sign. The more branches you run, the more it matters that it's done the same way every time.

This guide covers how to add a new branch, how to keep branch details consistent, when bulk upload and bulk verification help, and how to give your team the right access.

## A new branch means a new Business Profile

Each branch gets its own Business Profile on Google. Google's guidelines say: "Do not create more than one page for each location of your business, either in a single account or multiple accounts" ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en)).

The profile must be for a real place customers can visit. Google says to create the profile for your actual, real-world location, and virtual offices aren't eligible ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en)).

## Adding a branch, step by step

To add a new Business Profile, Google's steps are ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/2911778?hl=en)):

1. Go to `business.google.com/add`
2. Click **Add your business to Google**
3. Enter the branch details by following the on-screen instructions
4. Select a verification option and follow the steps

Google also reminds you to make sure the address is correct and meets its guidelines.

### If the branch is already on the map

Sometimes a customer adds your branch before you do. In that case, don't create a new profile. Claim the existing one. In Google Maps on a computer, search for the business name and city, select **Claim this business**, then **Manage now**, and pick a verification option ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/2911778?hl=en)).

## Keeping names and categories consistent

This is where chains go wrong most often. Google's guidelines are clear ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en)):

- **Real-world name only:** the name should match what customers see on your signage, website and stationery
- **No extra information:** adding taglines, neighbourhood names or hours to the name isn't permitted and can lead to suspension
- **No store codes in the name:** Google lists store codes explicitly as something a name must not include
- **One name per country:** all branches within the same country must use the same name, with exceptions such as genuinely distinct sub-brands
- **One shared primary category:** all branches of a business must share the one category that best represents it
- **As few categories as possible:** use the fewest categories that describe your core business

In practice, if your brand is "Elite Clinics", the Al Malqa branch on Google is "Elite Clinics", not "Elite Clinics Al Malqa Best Dental Clinic". The address and map pin tell people which branch it is.

## Adding many branches at once

If you're opening several branches or moving a whole chain into one account, Google offers bulk import through Business Profile Manager ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/4542428?hl=en)):

1. Prepare your spreadsheet. For a first import, download the template from the import screen
2. Fix duplicate or missing store codes before uploading
3. Sign in to Business Profile Manager
4. Choose to add a business, then import businesses, and select your file
5. Accepted formats are `.xls`, `.xlsx`, `.ods` and `.csv`
6. Fix any duplicate or unrecognised column headers, since they're ignored
7. Click to preview changes, then **Apply** to accept or **Cancel** to reject

### Required columns for each branch

Google says each branch in the sheet needs ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3370250?hl=en)):

- A unique store code
- A business name
- An address, plus latitude and longitude if the address can't be verified
- A primary phone number or website
- A primary category

### Watch your store codes

The store code is what ties each row to a specific branch. Google says codes must be different for each business and no longer than 64 characters ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3370250?hl=en)). It also warns that spreadsheet software can drop leading zeros, turning `0004` into `4`, which can create unintended new businesses. If the preview shows more new businesses than you expect, check the codes ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/4542428?hl=en)).

## Bulk verification for 10 or more branches

Instead of verifying each branch separately, Google offers bulk verification, with conditions ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/4490296?hl=en)):

- **10 or more profiles from the same business** in your spreadsheet
- Duplicate, suspended or disabled profiles **don't count** toward the minimum
- **Not available** to service-area businesses
- **Not available** to agencies with more than one business in one account
- **Only business owners or authorised representatives** may verify

To request it: sign in to Business Profile Manager, go to **Verifications**, then **Chain**, then **Start**, fill in the form and submit. Google notes that uploading a spreadsheet **doesn't send a verification request** on its own. You may also need to verify with a video, with an authorised representative at the branch. You'll get an email confirmation once bulk verification succeeds.

## Who owns the account? Owner and manager roles

Before adding anyone, know the difference between the two roles ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3403100?hl=en)):

- **Owner:** full control, including adding and removing users and deleting the profile
- **Manager:** edits info and photos and replies to reviews, but can't add or remove users or remove the profile
- **One primary owner:** a profile can have several owners but only one primary owner
- **One account per person:** each user needs their own Google Account, and Google Groups can't be added as owners or managers
- **7-day wait:** new owners and managers must wait 7 days before they can delete the profile, remove other users or transfer primary ownership

Our advice: keep primary ownership on a company-controlled account, not on an employee who might leave, and add branch managers and your marketing agency as Managers.

## Common mistakes that delay a new branch

- Creating a new profile when the branch is already on the map, which creates a duplicate
- Adding the neighbourhood or a tagline to the branch name
- Different primary categories across branches of the same chain
- An inaccurate address that doesn't match the branch entrance
- Losing leading zeros in store codes when saving the sheet
- Uploading a sheet with empty columns. Google says columns with a heading but no data will have existing info deleted ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3370250?hl=en))
- Relying on one employee's personal account as the only owner

## Checklist before any branch opens

- Search Google Maps to make sure the branch isn't already listed
- Use the exact chain name with no additions
- Pick the same primary category as your other branches
- Check the address and that the map pin sits on the right entrance
- Add the phone number and real opening hours
- Assign a new, unused store code
- Verify the branch, or include it in a bulk verification request if you qualify
- Add the branch manager with the Manager role

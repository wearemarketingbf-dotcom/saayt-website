During Ramadan most branches change their hours: later openings, closings after midnight and closed days over Eid. Customers rarely call to ask. They open Google and trust what it says. If Google says a branch is closed when it's open, you lose the visit. If it says open when the door is locked, you may lose the customer.

This guide shows how to set Ramadan and Eid hours on your Google Business Profile the way Google recommends, for one branch or for dozens.

## Why Ramadan hours on Google matter

Customers in Saudi Arabia actively search for shops' Ramadan hours, often typing the brand name followed by "Ramadan". Your regular year-round hours don't reflect the month.

Google lists hours among the things that help a profile perform. Its local ranking page recommends listing "your business hours, including special store hours", and says businesses with complete and accurate info are more likely to show up in local search results ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en)).

## Regular hours vs special hours

- **Regular hours:** your fixed weekly schedule, shown all year
- **Special hours:** a temporary change for specific dates such as holidays and events, without touching your regular hours

According to Google, special hours appear to customers **only on the dates you set**, and regular hours return automatically afterwards. For special hours to appear at all, the profile must be set to **"Open with main hours"** ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/6303076?hl=en)).

So the right approach for Ramadan is to add special hours for the days of the month, not to edit regular hours. Edit regular hours and you'll have to change every branch back by hand after Eid.

## Step by step: special hours for one branch

These steps come from Google's help page for the web ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/6303076?hl=en)):

1. Go to your Business Profile
2. Select **Edit profile**, then **Hours**
3. Under "Business hours", click **Edit** next to "Special hours"
4. Pick a date: confirm a suggested date, add a custom date or edit an existing one
5. If the branch is open, uncheck "Closed" and enter the hours. If it's closed, keep "Closed" checked
6. To add a second set of hours on the same day, use **More**, then **Add hours**
7. Select **Save**

## Updating every branch at once with a spreadsheet

With ten branches, entering thirty days by hand for each one means hundreds of entries. Google offers an alternative through the bulk upload spreadsheet in Business Profile Manager.

Google's rules ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/6303076?hl=en)):

- Enter hours in the **"Special hours"** column
- Use the date followed by hours, in 24-hour format `YYYY-MM-DD: HH:MM-HH:MM` or AM/PM format
- Use `x` for an all-day closure
- Separate multiple sets of hours with a comma
- If you're only updating special hours, include only the store code column and the special hours column

**Important:** Google says empty columns, or columns with a heading but no data, **will be erased**. Upload a sheet with an empty phone column and you may wipe phone numbers from every branch. Delete any column you don't need before uploading.

## Overnight Ramadan hours and how to split them

This is where most mistakes happen. Google says one set of special hours **can't exceed 24 hours**. To cover a period that crosses midnight, split it into two sets: one ending at the end of the first day and one starting at midnight on the next ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/6303076?hl=en)).

Example for a branch open 9 pm to 3 am (dates are illustrative only, not actual Ramadan dates):

```
2027-02-15: 21:00-00:00, 2027-02-16: 00:00-03:00
```

Google also notes that regular hours for the second day **are ignored**, so re-add any you want to keep as special hours. In practice, every Ramadan day carries two sets: the tail of last night and the start of tonight. A single day ends up looking like `00:00-03:00, 21:00-00:00`. If the branch also opens during the day, add that set to the same date with a comma.

## Eid closures: special hours or temporarily closed?

Google's rule is clear ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/6303076?hl=en)):

- **Closed for up to 6 days in a row:** use special hours and mark the days as closed
- **Closed for 7 or more days in a row, or for an unknown period:** mark the business as **Temporarily closed** instead

Google also suggests confirming your hours for official holidays even when they match your regular hours, so customers know your hours that day are accurate. If a branch works normal hours over Eid, confirm it rather than leaving customers to guess.

## Checklist: two weeks before Ramadan

- Collect each branch's Ramadan schedule from the branch manager in writing
- Check every profile is set to "Open with main hours"
- For each branch, decide whether its hours cross midnight. If yes, split them across two dates
- Prepare Eid hours separately and decide whether the closure is under 7 days or longer
- If you use the bulk sheet, delete every column you don't need and keep the store code
- Review the preview of changes before applying them
- Open Google Maps on your phone and check two or three branches
- Assign someone to recheck hours two days before Eid
- After Eid, confirm regular hours are back on every branch

## Common mistakes we see every season

### Editing regular hours instead of special hours

It feels faster, but you have to undo it by hand after Eid. With many branches, one or two get missed and keep showing Ramadan hours well into Shawwal.

### Entering 9 pm to 3 am as one set

Google's documented way to cover hours past midnight is to split the period across two dates, so every overnight entry should be split.

### Forgetting new branches

A branch that opened a month before Ramadan is often missing from last year's sheet. Check your branch list before uploading.

## Data we collect

### Data you give us on the website

When you request a demo or a free branch scan, we collect:

- Your name
- Company name
- Mobile number
- Number of branches
- Any extra details you choose to add to the form, such as your business name or Google Maps links to your branches for the scan

### Account and subscription data

When you use the platform, we may collect data about users authorised by the customer, such as name, email, role and permissions, as well as the billing and subscription data needed to provide the service.

### Google data we access with your permission

When you connect your Google Business Profile account to SAAYT, you grant us access through Google's secure sign-in (OAuth) to the following data, only as far as needed to provide the service:

- Basic Google account information needed for sign-in, such as name and email address
- The locations and branches linked to the account and their details, such as name, address, opening hours, phone number, category and description
- Photos published on branch profiles
- Reviews, reviewer names as shown on Google, and replies to reviews
- Performance insights that Google provides for the profile, where enabled in the service

We never ask for or store your Google password.

### Technical data

When you visit the website or use the platform, we may collect limited technical data such as IP address, browser and device type, and usage and error logs, to run and secure the service. We use cookies that are strictly necessary for the website to work. We do not use non-essential analytics or marketing cookies without your consent.

## How we use data and our lawful basis

| Purpose | Data | Lawful basis |
|---|---|---|
| Responding to your demo or free scan request and contacting you about it | Form data | Your request and pre-contract steps taken at your request |
| Providing the platform and managing your account, subscription and billing | Account and subscription data | Performance of a contract |
| Managing your branches on Google: updating hours, details and photos, showing reviews, and suggesting replies that are published only after your approval | Google data | Performance of a contract and your explicit authorisation through Google |
| Securing the platform, preventing fraud and fixing errors | Technical data | Legitimate interest and legal obligations |
| Sending marketing messages or offers | Name and mobile number | Your separate consent, which you can withdraw at any time |
| Complying with laws and lawful official requests | As needed | Legal obligation |

We do not use data for a purpose different from those listed above without first informing you and getting your consent where the law requires it.

## Review replies: a person approves first

SAAYT may suggest text for replies to customer reviews. **No reply is published on Google until a person authorised by you has reviewed and approved it.** The approving user remains responsible for the content of the published reply. The platform does not make fully automated decisions that have legal effects on you.

## Limited Use of Google data

SAAYT's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

- We use Google data only to provide and improve the user-facing features of the platform, such as managing branch details, photos, reviews and replies
- We do not sell Google data or transfer it to advertisers, data brokers or any third party for advertising purposes
- We do not use Google data to serve ads, for retargeting, or to determine creditworthiness or for lending
- We transfer Google data only as needed to provide or improve the service, to comply with applicable law, for security purposes, or as part of a merger or acquisition with your explicit prior consent
- Our staff do not read Google data unless you give explicit consent for a specific case (such as a support request, or the managed service if you subscribe to it), for security purposes, to comply with applicable law, or where the data is aggregated and anonymised for internal operations
- We comply with the Google Business Profile APIs policies, including the limits on how much content received from Google we store and for how long

## Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only in these cases:

- **Service providers acting for us:** such as hosting, cloud infrastructure, customer relationship management, messaging and payments, under contracts that require them to protect the data and use it only for the stated purpose

- **Authorities:** where required by law or in response to a lawful request
- **Restructuring or acquisition:** in a merger or sale, subject to this policy and the law

## Data breaches

If a breach of or unauthorised access to personal data occurs, we act on it immediately. We notify the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA) within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, as required by the Implementing Regulations, and we notify you without undue delay if the incident may cause you harm, explaining what happened, the steps we are taking and what you can do to protect yourself.

## Your rights

Under the PDPL you have the right to:

- **Be informed:** know the legal basis and purpose for collecting your data
- **Access:** see your data and get a copy of it in a clear format
- **Correction:** ask us to correct, complete or update your data
- **Destruction:** ask us to destroy data we no longer need, unless a law requires us to keep it
- **Withdraw consent:** withdraw your consent at any time where processing is based on consent, without affecting processing done before withdrawal
- **Restrict processing:** ask us to restrict processing while the accuracy of your data is being checked, in the cases set out in the Regulations

**How to make a request:** email hello@saayt.app [to confirm] and describe your request. We may ask you to verify your identity first. We respond within 30 days, and we may extend this by a further 30 days where the Regulations allow, telling you why.

**Disconnecting Google:** you can disconnect your Google account at any time from the platform settings, or remove SAAYT's access from your Google Account security page at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

**Complaints:** if you believe we have breached the law, you have the right to complain to the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA). We would welcome the chance to address your concern first.
